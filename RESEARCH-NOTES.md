# Research notes (not shown on the site)

The site only shows facts and credits that could be confirmed. These notes list what is still open, so you can research it later. Each item is also in that sleeve's `verify[]` in `src/data/sleeves.json`. If you confirm a credit, put the name in `designer` or `photographer`, cite the source in `sources[]`, and remove the item from `verify[]`. The site will then show it automatically.

## How credits were confirmed

- **Printed sleeve credits** come from Discogs listings of specific pressings (usually the original Nigerian one). The source link in each detail view points to that exact release.
- **Album credits** come from Wikipedia personnel sections that cite the album's back cover.
- **Biographies** come from Wikipedia, Archivi.ng (Peter Obe) and the Daily Maverick (Lemi Ghariokwu).

## Corrections made in this pass

- **Sorrow Tears and Blood (1977).** The original Kalakuta sleeve (KK 001) credits the front cover to Okanlawon Banjoko and the photograph to Femi Osunla. It is not a Lemi Ghariokwu cover; his Soweto-inspired design for this record was the one Fela rejected.
- **Zombie** is dated 1977, not 1976. The original Coconut pressing (PMLP 1003) is printed ℗ 1977, and Wikipedia's Fela Kuti article agrees.
- **Face 2 Face (2004).** The Kennis Music CD (KMCD 037) credits its design to Lemi Ghariokwu.
- **Two sleeves were replaced** because they couldn't be documented:
  - Plantashun Boiz, *Body and Soul*: year and label unconfirmable. Replaced by Wande Coal, *Mushin 2 Mo' Hits* (2009).
  - Tekno, "Pana": a single with no documented cover, year or label. Replaced by Wizkid, *Sounds from the Other Side* (2017).
